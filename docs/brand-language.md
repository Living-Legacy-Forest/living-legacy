# Brand language

How the website sounds and which words it uses. Condensed from the _Living Legacy Language &
Tone Guide_ (November 2026). It applies to every customer-facing word: page copy, CMS content,
alt text, meta titles and descriptions, form labels, button text, emails and error messages.

When the guide and this file disagree, the guide wins; update this file.

## Tone

**What we do is radical. How we talk about it is ordinary.** Approachable in manner, bold in
stance. Write about the life that was lived, not the loss. Tagline: **Live On.**

| Principle                       | Write                                                                    | Not                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Life, not loss                  | "Remembered by their life, not their loss."                              | "In loving memory of those we have lost."                     |
| Plain and warm                  | "Your family will always have a place to visit."                         | "We facilitate perpetual memorialisation solutions."          |
| Confident, never pushy          | "Prices are listed below."                                               | "Limited spaces, enquire now before they're gone."            |
| Serious, never sombre           | "A ceremony to place your mother's ashes and plant her tree."            | "A solemn final farewell."                                    |
| Show the science, don't lecture | "The treatment makes ashes safe for soil, so a tree can grow from them." | "A paramagnetic soil formula that neutralises pH and sodium." |

### Tone by reader

| Mode         | Reader                                                | Sound                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-planning | Arranging their own or a family member's future       | Uplifting, forward-looking, in control. Say what they will leave behind.                          |
| At-need      | A family whose person has just died                   | Calm and brief. Few decisions, exactly what happens next. No urgency. Sentences under 12 words.   |
| Considering  | Comparing us with a cemetery or funeral director      | Unhurried, led by evidence. Answer directly. Compare with the industry, never a named competitor. |
| Trade        | Crematoria, funeral directors, celebrants, solicitors | Brisk and practical: logistics, process, correct terms.                                           |

Set against the traditional funeral industry (cemeteries, headstones, urns) without mocking it.
"Trees, not headstones" works. Mocking what someone chose for their family does not.

## The six names

They tell one story: the **EarthSApHE™ Treatment** makes **EarthSApHE™ Ashes**. These are placed
during a **Legacy Infusion Ceremony**, in **Legacy Ground**, in a **Living Legacy Forest**, and
the family holds a **Living Legacy Title** to that ground.

| Name                              | Means                                                                                                                                           | Never write                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| EarthSApHE™ Treatment             | The patented treatment that makes cremated ashes safe for soil, so they feed a tree                                                             | SoilSAFE, SSAFE, Living Legacy Formula, Living Legacy Liquid, paramagnetic soil, the process, the formula |
| EarthSApHE™ Ashes                 | Ashes after the treatment and before planting. Use with the person: "Mum's EarthSApHE™ Ashes"                                                   | treated ashes, soil-safe ashes, living memorial, product, compound, mixture, remains, cremains            |
| Legacy Infusion Ceremony          | The gathering where the ashes are placed and the tree takes them up                                                                             | infusion (alone), planting, burial, interment, service                                                    |
| Legacy Ground                     | The family's own located, protected, permanently maintained space where their tree grows. What they buy and inherit                             | plot (never), lot, allotment, spot, grave, resting place, "a tree" as the purchase                        |
| Living Legacy Title / Entitlement | The lasting, transferable right to Legacy Ground. **Title** in conversation and page copy; **Entitlement** on certificates, contracts and wills | licence, lease, membership, subscription, "protected care forever" as a name                              |
| Living Legacy Forest              | One of our places. "Site" only in technical or operational text                                                                                 | sanctuary, botanical, memorial garden, memorial park, gardens, cemetery, location (in customer copy)      |

"Infusion" is no longer a pricing tier name. Tiers describe how many people a tree holds.

### Forest names

Always **Living Legacy + place name**: Living Legacy Mornington, Living Legacy Wellington Dam,
Living Legacy Logan. Never the place name alone, never the place before the brand.

**Mornington Green is retired.** The Victorian forest is **Living Legacy Mornington**. A link to
the old domain may stay until that site migrates, but the name does not appear in copy.

### Writing EarthSApHE™

- Spell it exactly: `EarthSApHE`. Never EarthSAPHE, Earthsaphe, Earth SApHE or EARTHSAPHE, even
  in an all-caps heading (CSS `text-transform: uppercase` breaks it, so don't apply it there).
- Add `™` as a small superscript (`<sup>™</sup>`) on its first use on a page, and every time in a
  heading, on packaging or on a certificate.
- Always pair it with a noun (Treatment, Ashes). Not a verb ("treated with EarthSApHE™", never
  "EarthSApHE'd"), never plural.

## Style

- Australian English: licence, colour, organise, centre.
- Capitalise the six names. Lower case for tree, forest (alone), family, ceremony (alone).
- Sentence case for headings. All caps only for short labels.
- Short sentences: under 20 words, under 12 in at-need copy.
- Show prices. Never "enquire for pricing".
- Numerals for numbers: 8 people, 100 years.
- Use the person's name or relationship ("your mum", "David"), not "the deceased" or "the loved
  one".
- "Died" is the plain word in written copy. No euphemisms.
- House rules from the agency: no em or en dashes, no exclamation marks in information copy.

| Write                           | Instead of                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| your mum, your husband, David   | the deceased, the departed, remains, loved one |
| their tree, their Legacy Ground | grave, resting place, final resting place      |
| ashes, EarthSApHE™ Ashes        | cremains, remains                              |
| life, legacy, what grows next   | loss, closure, farewell, final                 |

Other glossary terms: **Legacy Planner** (not salesperson, consultant, agent), **celebrant** (not
officiant), **partner** (not reseller, affiliate), **pre-planning** (not pre-need, pre-purchase),
**tree** (not "memorial tree" as a product name). **Living Legacy Earth** is the corporate entity
and never appears on family-facing pages. Never abbreviate Living Legacy to "LL" in copy.

### Never in customer copy

plot · grave · cemetery (except when comparing) · remains · cremains · the deceased · final
resting place · paramagnetic · formula · liquid · limited availability · act now · SoilSAFE

## Needs head office approval

Don't invent these, in copy or in code (labels, slugs, enum values):

- new names, nicknames or short forms for the six names
- offers, discounts, bundles or limited-time promotions
- science claims beyond what is written here
- new taglines or campaign lines

## Approved copy

Use as written. Where a field has a length limit, pick the version that fits; don't shorten one
yourself.

- **Tagline:** Live On.
- **Headlines:** Trees, not headstones. / Remembered by their life, not their loss. / A place
  that belongs to your family, for good.
- **One-liner (under 15 words):** the guide's text reads "Living Legacy tran ashes into a living
  tree, in ground your family keeps forever." The verb is a typo; confirm it before using.
- **Social bio (under 150 characters):** A tree, not a headstone. Legacy Ground in Living Legacy
  Forests across Australia, held by your family for generations. Live On.
- **Partner line:** Now offering the EarthSApHE™ Treatment, which makes ashes safe to grow a
  living tree.

**Boilerplate, 25 words:** Living Legacy is a new kind of memorial. Our patented EarthSApHE™
Treatment lets ashes grow a living tree, in Legacy Ground a family keeps for generations.

**Boilerplate, 50 words:** Living Legacy offers families a living alternative to the cemetery.
Our patented EarthSApHE™ Treatment transforms cremated ashes to make them safe for soil and able
to feed a tree. A Legacy Infusion Ceremony places the ashes in a family's own Legacy Ground,
where the tree grows on as part of a protected Living Legacy Forest, forever.

**Boilerplate, 100 words:** Living Legacy is built on a simple idea: people should be remembered
by their life, not their loss. Cremated ashes on their own harm soil. Our patented EarthSApHE™
Treatment changes that, transforming ashes into nutrient rich and environmentally safe so they
are able to feed a tree. Family and friends gather at a Legacy Infusion Ceremony to place them in
Legacy Ground, a family's own protected space in a Living Legacy Forest. Unlike a cemetery
licence, a Living Legacy Title doesn't expire. It is recorded, passed down and added to, so the
tree and the place stay with the family for generations. Live On.

The longer "About Living Legacy" (about 300 words) and "For partners" (about 120 words) pieces
are in the guide itself; copy them from there.
